# Pulse & Protocol mobile app (v13) and brand

## Mission
High-quality, clinically rigorous digital education tools for nursing students and ER nurses.

## Mobile app (v13), state as of Oct 2026
- Single-file React artifact: Test, Index, Calculators
- 41 cases across 14 sections, 186 index entries, 549 nursing intervention bullets, 36 dosing entries with a verified/unverified provenance model
- Look: navy gradient header, soft gray page, white rounded cards, coral accents, three-tab navy bottom bar. Fonts: Space Grotesk, Inter Tight, JetBrains Mono.
- Repo: `Pulse-Protocol` (offline ER dosing reference and quiz app)

## Open items
- Convert about 33 of 41 flat cases to richer mechanic sets
- Formulary verification for unverified dosing entries
- Export the 549 bullets as a reviewable checklist
- Pediatric age-banding review

## Business side
- Etsy storefront shoprn14.etsy.com with digital PDF products. See `04-content-commerce`.
- Pinterest is the traffic engine with a no-face, no-voice model
- GitHub Pages hosts HTML tools. Note: private repos need a paid plan for Pages.
- Pin Content Generator uses the Anthropic API (claude-sonnet-4-6). Expanding it for Pinterest bulk upload.
- STEMI quickbook: Canva is the right tool. Excalidraw output wasn't polished enough.
- Competitor research (for example NursingKamp) shaped product design. Denser, polished "survival guide" visuals outperform simple ones in the nursing Etsy market.
- Side-gig priorities (Oct 28): freelance nurse-led clinical content for ed-tech and health publishers first, Etsy over Shopify for info products, nursing CE courses, printable cheat sheets with matching LinkedIn posts.

TikTok, Reels, Shorts. Text and caption driven, no face, no voice. Goal is audience for ad revenue and sponsorships. See `04-content-commerce`.
